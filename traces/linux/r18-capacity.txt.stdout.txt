  ladder (command bytes, data = command - 72): ["0x800", "0x1000", "0x2000", "0x2be8", "0x4000"]
  each step is ONE command whose header is memcpy(0,0,0): no destination, no source, no
  length. It writes nothing anywhere, and the reply echoes the appended data back for
  comparison. The ladder stops at the first step that does not come back intact.
