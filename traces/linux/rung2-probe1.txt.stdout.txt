## what the result decides
  * AUSB cfg0/cfg1 non-zero and stable -> `apple,cfg0-device`/`cfg1-device` for the
    ausbphy0 node, which is what unblocks the USB gadget console (ttyGS0).
  * last address that round-trips -> the DRAM extent. Declaring MORE than exists
    corrupts rather than panics (LOADER-FILL.md §2), so this is measured, not assumed.
  * a failure at the first address means the ROM's EL3 translation does not reach DRAM
    at all, which is PLAN B in DESIGN.md §11.1 and stops the design.
